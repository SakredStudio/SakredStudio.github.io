## Full description
Swaiyu is the K-pop fan app for every group you stan.

⏳ COUNT DOWN TO EVERY COMEBACK
See upcoming releases for the groups you follow, with a big countdown on your home screen and a one-tap pre-order link.

💜 YOUR FAN CARD, YOUR STORY
Add your fan name, your bias group and the year you started stanning. Share it as a story or square image in seconds.

🎂 NEVER MISS A BIRTHDAY
Member birthdays across 100+ groups, with today's celebrations and the next ones coming up.

🎟 TICKETS AND PREP IN ONE PLACE
Jump to live ticket listings for your groups, save your show for a countdown, and work through a concert prep checklist. Learn fanchants before the big night.

🤖 ASK FANI ANYTHING
FANI is your K-pop helper for concerts, merch, photocards and fan terms.

🎨 PICK YOUR VIBE
Night, Pastel, or a theme in your group's own color.

No account needed. Your groups, Fan Card and checklist stay on your phone.
